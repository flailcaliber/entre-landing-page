FAQs

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete Account. On a computer, it's in Settings under My account. If you can't sign in, email us from the address on your account and we'll take care of it.

How do I block or report someone?

On the Friends tab, open the … menu next to their name and choose Block or Report. Blocking ends your friendship, cancels plans between just the two of you, and stops them from sending you requests, invites or notifications.

Can I invite someone who doesn't have Entre?

Yes. When you plan a lunch, add them as a guest and send them their link by text or email. They answer in their browser, with no account and no download.

How does booking a table work?

When you pick a spot, Entre opens the restaurant's own booking page, such as Resy or OpenTable, with the day, time and party size filled in. You book there, so you change or cancel it there too. If your plans change, Entre reminds you to update the table.

How do I turn off emails or notifications?

For emails, open Settings in Entre and turn off Email notifications. For phone notifications, tap Notifications in Entre's Settings, which opens your iPhone's settings for Entre.

Does Entre cost anything?

No. Entre is free on iPhone and on the web at app.entre.nyc.

How is my information used?

Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect and why, and our Terms of Service cover the rest. To get a copy of your data or have it deleted, email us from the address on your account.
