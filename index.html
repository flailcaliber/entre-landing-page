What we are
What we are

A small app for the lunch you keep saying you'll get.

Entre is how you actually get lunch on the calendar with people you already know. Friends, coworkers, the person from your old job you keep meaning to text. You pick who's coming, everyone says what they're in the mood for, and Entre finds restaurants roughly halfway between you, filtered by cuisine, budget, and how much time you've got.

It isn't for meeting strangers, and it isn't a networking app. We built it because our own workday plans kept falling apart in the back-and-forth of figuring out where to go. Entre handles that part, so "we should grab lunch" turns into a table on Thursday at 1.

Your friend works six blocks away. You haven't seen them since March.
